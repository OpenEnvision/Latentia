Latentia is OpenEnvision’s platform for AI image art, short films, and human–machine collaboration. A place to discover imagined worlds and the people who bring them into view.

Latentia 隶属于 OpenEnvision，关注 AI 图像艺术、微电影与人机共创。在这里，尚未抵达现实的影像与创造它们的人，一同进入视野。
