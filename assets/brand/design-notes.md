# Latentia · The latent frame

“Where images wait to be seen.” / “万象未现之境。”

The symbol pairs a dominant L with a detached upper-right corner. Their negative space suggests an image frame that has not fully appeared. The contrast between the grounded L and the smaller floating corner is intentional.

Latentia is the AIGC platform of OpenEnvision.

## Files

- `latentia-mark.svg`: sage symbol on transparent background.
- `latentia-mark-ink.svg`: graphite symbol for light backgrounds.
- `latentia-mark-mono.svg`: ivory symbol for dark backgrounds.
- `latentia-logo.svg`: sage symbol and ivory wordmark, transparent background.
- `latentia-logo-ink.svg`: graphite signature for light backgrounds.
- `latentia-logo-mono.svg`: ivory signature for dark backgrounds.
- `latentia-mark.png`, `latentia-mark-ink.png`: 1024 px transparent exports.
- `latentia-logo.png`, `latentia-logo-ink.png`: 2400 px transparent exports.
- `latentia-favicon.svg`, `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-32.png`: graphite tile with sage symbol.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`: 180 × 180 icon.
- `latentia-brandboard.svg`, `latentia-brandboard.png`: identity presentation.
- `latentia-concept.png`: original imagegen exploration, retained separately from the clean vector masters.

All signature SVG lettering is outlined; it does not require an installed font. The wordmark uses Arial Bold outlines with −0.058 em tracking, matching the website’s existing typography. The website retains live accessible text beside the symbol. The symbol itself is editable geometry in a 64 × 64 coordinate system.

## Use

Use sage `#D8EDAF` and ivory `#EEEDE6` on graphite `#10110F`; use the ink variant on light surfaces. Keep a clear area of at least 8 symbol units around the mark. Use a 32 px image box in the desktop navigation and the supplied favicon for browser tabs. Keep the original proportions and two separated pieces. Avoid shadows, gradients, squeezing or rotating the mark.

The identity has been applied to the homepage, recommendation page, links page, footers, artwork dialog, About dialog, favicon and touch icon. The previous `assets/favicon.svg` remains available as the legacy icon.

## Creation record

Initial exploration used the built-in `image_gen` tool with transparency enabled; no API-key or CLI fallback was used. The final production symbol was reconstructed as clean, flat SVG geometry from that direction. Raster exports were rendered from these SVG masters. The signature lettering was converted to paths using macOS CoreText, rather than bundling a font file.

Exact generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE exceptionally refined original symbol for LATENTIA, an editorial AI art and experimental film platform belonging to OpenEnvision. Concept: an image waiting to become visible, an unclosed revelation window, the first letter L. Design a bold minimal abstract L-shaped portal made of exactly TWO flat geometric pieces: a dominant lower-left L form with an optically rounded outside corner, and a smaller detached upper-right curved corner. Their negative space implies a square image plane waiting to be revealed. Make the two pieces noticeably asymmetrical, intentional, balanced, memorable and sophisticated like a contemporary art institution identity. This is a symbol only, NO text or letters written out, NO presentation board, NO mockup. Pale sage-lime #d8edaf only, full opaque solid ink on a genuinely transparent background. The L should read through silhouette and negative space, not a literal typeset letter. Strong filled geometry, generous central negative space, slightly softened outer contour, precise inner edges, recognisable at 16-32 pixels. One centred mark fills about 75% of the square canvas with generous equal clear space. Avoid gradients, shadows, textures, 3D, glass, outlines, grids, multiple options, eye symbols, cameras, infinity loops, AI sparkles, generic four-corner scan marks, decorations, watermarks, and any background colour.
